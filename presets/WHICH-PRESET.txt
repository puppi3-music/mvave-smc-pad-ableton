Load each file into this preset slot in MidiSuite (M-Vave's editor):

  Preset 1   preset1-launcher-mixer.spc    clip launcher, PAD BANK -> mixer
  Preset 2   preset2-sequencer-drums.spc   step sequencer, PAD BANK -> drum pads
  Preset 3   preset3-keys.spc              scale keyboard + device knobs, PAD BANK -> key/scale
  Preset 4   preset4-chords.spc            chords + device knobs, PAD BANK -> key/scale

On the controller, hold Shift and press pad 1, 2, 3 or 4 to switch presets.
Full setup (scripts and Live's ports): docs/smc-pad-guide.html, "Setup".
